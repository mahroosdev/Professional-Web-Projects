Privacy Policy
Privacy Notice
100% local, zero tracking
🛡️ Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in FOLIO is stored locally on your device — never on a server. We designed a 3-layer storage system so you always stay in control. Your notes and tasks are never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with any third party.

How Your Data Is Saved
💾
Layer 1 — Local File Best
Link a .json file on your device via Settings → Data Storage. Every change auto-saves within 1 second. Safe even if you clear all browser data. Available on Chrome & Edge.
📦
Layer 2 — Export / Import Backup
Works on all browsers. Export a folio_backup.json anytime and restore it later. A confirmation shows your note & task count before anything is overwritten.
⚡
Layer 3 — Browser Cache
localStorage is always active as the instant working cache so the app stays fast. Used automatically — no setup needed.
No account needed — Start immediately without an email or password.
No analytics — We don't track your usage, clicks, or behaviour.
No cookies — We use local storage only, zero tracking scripts.
Works offline — Full functionality without an internet connection.
Offline Connections
🔤
Offline Fonts
Typefaces use polished local system font stacks. No font request is made, and no notes or tasks ever leave this device.
📧
Bug Reports EmailJS
Sends through EmailJS when credentials and internet are available. With placeholder keys, the form stays local and shows setup guidance.
Caution — Browser Cache

If you are using Layer 3 only (no linked file), clearing "Website Data" or "Browser Cache" will delete your notes and tasks. Linking a local file (Layer 1) or regularly exporting a backup (Layer 2) fully protects your data from this risk.
